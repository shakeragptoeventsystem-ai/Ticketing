Shakerag PTO

Event check-in and volunteer tools

The Shakerag PTO Event App helps approved volunteers check families in, verify event registrations, record item fulfillment, and perform limited event administration. It is used for Shakerag PTO events and is not a public ticket marketplace.

Volunteers sign in with Google. Access to scanning and administration is limited by the PTO's volunteer roster and assigned role. Families can use the unique self-check link included with their PTO QR pass to review their own event items and status.
