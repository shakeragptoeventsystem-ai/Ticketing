Shakerag PTO Event App

Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

This policy describes information handled by the Shakerag PTO Event App, including its volunteer scanner, administration tools, and family QR self-check page. The app is operated by Shakerag PTO for its event activities.

Information handled

  • Volunteer sign-in: Google provides the signed-in volunteer's email address. The PTO roster may also contain a volunteer's display name, role, and active status.
  • Event and family records: the app uses family names and contact email addresses, event registrations, item quantities, session assignments, QR identifiers, and check-in or fulfillment status maintained for PTO events.
  • Operational records: the system records volunteer actions such as check-ins, item fulfillment, administrative changes, and email delivery status. These records can include timestamps, volunteer identity, event and family references, and action details.
  • Technical requests: Google, GitHub Pages, and the app's Google sign-in and QR-scanning library providers may process technical information such as browser requests, device/browser details, and network data under their own policies.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate volunteers, decide whether their account is approved and what role it has, administer PTO events, send QR passes and event reminders, show families their registration and check-in status, and maintain operational and audit records.

Family QR links

A family self-check link contains a unique QR identifier in the URL. Anyone who obtains that link may be able to view the associated family name and event registration, items, and status. Treat the QR code and self-check link like an event pass; do not post or forward them publicly. Contact the PTO if a link is shared unintentionally.

Service providers and disclosure

The app uses Google services for sign-in, hosting the backend, email, and spreadsheet data storage, and GitHub Pages to host the volunteer web page. It also loads the Google sign-in library and QR-scanning library from their providers. Those providers process information as needed to deliver their services and under their own privacy terms. The PTO does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the service.

Storage and retention

Event and operational records are maintained in PTO-controlled Google services. Event history may be retained for administration, reporting, and audit purposes. A fixed deletion schedule has not been established in this policy; contact the PTO with a correction or deletion request. Some records may need to be retained to document event operations.

Access and security

Volunteer access is limited through the PTO roster and assigned roles. The PTO uses administrative and technical controls intended to protect records, but no internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Volunteers should use only their approved account, protect QR links, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

Children and family information

The app is operated by adults for PTO event administration. It does not ask children to create accounts or sign in. Family event records may include household registration and admission quantities.

Contact

For questions or requests about information handled by the app, email shakeragptoeventsystem@gmail.com.
